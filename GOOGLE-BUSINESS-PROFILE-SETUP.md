# Google Business Profile Setup Guide for King City Disposal

## Why This Is Your #1 SEO Priority

Google Business Profile (GBP) is responsible for **60%+ of local search visibility**. When someone searches "dumpster rental near me" or "dumpster rental mount vernon il", the Map Pack (those 3 businesses with the map) gets more clicks than the regular search results.

**You MUST complete this before anything else.**

---

## Step 1: Create/Claim Your Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Add your business to Google"
4. Search for "King City Disposal" to see if it already exists
   - If it exists: Claim it
   - If not: Create new

## Step 13: Verification

Google will verify your business via:
- **Postcard**: Takes 5-14 days
- **Phone**: Sometimes available for service businesses
- **Video**: Google may ask you to record a video of your business

Complete verification ASAP - you can't appear in search without it.

---

## Step 14: Get Your Place ID

After verification:
1. Go to [Google's Place ID Finder](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id)
2. Search for "King City Disposal"
3. Copy the Place ID
4. Add it to your config.js file
